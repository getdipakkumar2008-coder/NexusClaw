# Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, credit others' work, and keep discussion on the technical merits.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised attention, sustained disruption, publishing private information, or spamming.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban participants. Report concerns privately to the maintainers in `MAINTAINERS.md`. <!-- TODO(owner): add a conduct contact. -->
